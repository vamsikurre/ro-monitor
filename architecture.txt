1. TERRACE & ROOF TOP SUBSYSTEM (RS485 DAISY-CHAIN BUS + 12V DC DISTRIBUTION)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Physical Layout:
- Central Skid & Dosing Tank in RO Room (dosing sensor lands straight on the hub, ~1 m).
- Raw Water Tank (RWT) and Treated Water Tank (TWT) on Roof Top in opposite directions.
- Battery Room with Exhaust Fan & Climate monitoring, spliced mid-chain between the RO Room and the Roof.

Electrical Topology: 100% Strictly Linear Daisy-Chain, single outbound walk
Chain order: 0x00 HUB -> 0x04 BATTERY ROOM -> 0x03 TWT -> 0x02 RWT
Terminators: 0x00 (hub) and 0x02 (RWT, end of bus). No loopback, no junction box.
Dosing tank (~1 m from hub): AJ-SR04M wired DIRECT to the ESP32, not an RS485 node.
  Address 0x01 is retired. See docs/WIRING.md section 13.
